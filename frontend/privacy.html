1. Who we are

AI Resume Analyzer (the "Service") is owned and operated by LazyOrangeCat, an independent project based in Australia ("LazyOrangeCat", "we", "us" or "our"). We decide how and why your personal data is used for the Service, which makes us the data controller.

You can contact us about privacy at any time at snapstries@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We only collect what the Service needs to work. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email address to use the analyzer.

3. How and why we use it

  • To analyze your resume and show you the results. This is the service you ask for when you upload. We rely on providing that requested service as our legal basis.
  • To keep the Service secure and working. We use technical data to deliver the site, limit abuse (for example, rate limits), and find and fix errors. This is in our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • To reply to you when you contact us, because you asked us to.

We don't use your data for advertising or marketing, we don't sell or rent it, and we don't use it to train AI models. The ATS score and suggestions are informational. We don't use them to make any decision about you, and we don't share them with employers.

4. Who processes it

The Service runs entirely on Amazon Web Services (AWS), which processes data only on our behalf and under our instructions, as set out in the AWS Customer Agreement and the AWS Data Processing Addendum. These are the AWS services we use:

  • Amazon S3 stores your uploaded file privately.
  • AWS Lambda runs the steps that process your upload.
  • Amazon Textract reads the text in your file. We have opted out of AWS using content processed by its AI services to improve those services.
  • Amazon Bedrock (with the Amazon Nova model) writes your analysis. AWS states that Bedrock doesn't store prompts or outputs, doesn't share them with model providers, and doesn't use them to train models.
  • Amazon DynamoDB stores your results.
  • Amazon API Gateway and Amazon CloudFront deliver the site and handle requests.
  • Amazon CloudWatch keeps system logs and alerts.

The website's fonts and code are served from our own site, so no other company receives your data when you visit. We will only disclose personal data if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

5. Where it is processed

Your data is stored and processed in the AWS US East (N. Virginia) region in the United States. If you use the Service from another country, including Saudi Arabia, the European Union or the United Kingdom, your data is transferred to the United States.

We protect these transfers by keeping data to the minimum needed and encrypting it in transit and at rest. Storage is private and access is restricted, data is deleted automatically after 30 days, and AWS's contractual data protection commitments apply. These commitments include the standard contractual clauses in the AWS Data Processing Addendum.

7. How we protect it

  • All connections use HTTPS (TLS).
  • Files and results are encrypted at rest.
  • Storage buckets block all public access. Uploads use one-time links that expire quickly and only allow uploading, not reading.
  • Each part of the system has only the permissions it needs.
  • Rate limits and monitoring alerts help detect misuse.

Your results can be opened by anyone who has your resume ID, so don't share it if you want your results to stay private. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the relevant authority and, where required, you, within the time limits set by law.

8. Your rights

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. Depending on where you live (for example, under Australia's Privacy Act, Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, the EU or UK GDPR, or your local law), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting earlier use;
  • complain to a privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) in Australia, the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) in Saudi Arabia, or the authority where you live. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

To use any of these rights, email us at snapstries@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, please include your resume ID (shown with your results) so we can find your data. We may ask for something to confirm the data is yours. We will reply within 30 days. The quickest way to have your data deleted early is to email us your resume ID.

9. Storage in your browser

We don't use cookies. The site saves a few settings in your browser's local storage so it remembers what you chose:

  • siteTheme: light or dark mode.
  • cursorStyle: your cursor choice.
  • devLink: only used by the site owner, to show the admin link.

These settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

10. Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 16. Please don't upload a resume belonging to someone under 16. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
